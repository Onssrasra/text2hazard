# Project Requirements

## Project Pitch

**Date:** 28 August 2026

**Duration:** approximately 15 minutes for a multi-person project.

The presentation should cover:

- Brief introduction to the topic
- Research goal and research questions
- State of the art
- Research gap
- Choice of models/workflow
- Proposed methodology
- Preprocessing
- First findings from data exploration
- Optional preliminary model results
- Expected results
- Challenges and limitations

## Final Submission

**Deadline:** 15 October 2026, 23:59

The final submission consists of:

1. Project report
2. Public GitHub codebase
3. Data or sufficient information to reproduce the dataset

For a group of two students, the report must contain at least **3,000 words**.

The report should cover:

- Introduction and motivation
- State of the art and research gap
- Dataset and methodology
- AI models and hyperparameters
- Results and evaluation
- Experimental comparison
- Discussion
- Limitations
- Conclusion

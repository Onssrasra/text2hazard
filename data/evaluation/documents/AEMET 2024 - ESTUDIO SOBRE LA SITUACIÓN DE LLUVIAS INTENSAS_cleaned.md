# AEMET 2024 - ESTUDIO SOBRE LA SITUACIÓN DE LLUVIAS INTENSAS

## 1. Introducción.

Floods associated with torrential precipitation are one of the natural disasters that has the greatest impact each year throughout our planet. The great number of human losses, the destruction of infrastructure and the enormous economic losses that they bring are some of its most relevant impacts. Between October 28 and November 4, 2024, a long episode of intense rains, locally torrential and persistent, occurred in our country, which affected to a greater extent the provinces of the Mediterranean, adjacent provinces of the communities of Castilla-La Mancha and Aragon, as well as part of the south-western quadrant.

## 1. Introducción.

Among all the days of the period covered by this study, he highlighted the 29th of October, when very serious impacts occurred, especially in the province of Valencia. That day, the weather factor, extraordinarily extreme, was combined with the hydrological factor and triggered the most tragic natural event in the recent history of our country. 223 people lost their lives (3 still missing at the time of this study) and the economic impact on the main economic sectors would exceed 13 billion euros, according to the Chamber of Valencia (Camera V. 2024).To this figure, the extraordinary losses and deterioration of the infrastructure, as well as the material damage suffered by thousands of families of the populations affected by the floods, the latter translated in 3.5 billion euros, according to estimates of the Consortium of Insurance Compensation (Consorcio CS. 2024). Also this day there were areas of the provinces of Albacete, Cuenca and Malaga where the loss of human life occurred due to the sudden increase of channels, as well as numerous material and infrastructure damages.

## 1. Introducción.

The presence of a dana towards the Strait area and the transport of moisture from a very warm Mediterranean to the Peninsula caused a great atmospheric instability that led to the development of powerful stormy systems that in the area of Turís (Valencia) left records in our country of precipitation accumulated in one, six and twelve hours. The development of the weather situation and its subsequent evolution are explained in chapter 2 of this report, while in chapter 3 we show a description of the main stormy systems that were generated throughout the days during which the situation of strong instability was maintained.Chapter 4 shows a daily chronology of how rainfall was occurring, highlighting those autonomous communities where rainfall caused the most impact. This chapter also describes the weather warnings that were issued throughout these days for the most affected areas. Finally, chapter 5 shows a short comparative study of rainfall on the 29th day focused on the province of Valencia with respect to three other historical situations of strong socioeconomic impact in this province since 1950.

## 2. Descripción sinóptica.

On Thursday, 24 October, a large-scale vacuum located in the middle of the North Atlantic had two very balanced jets of entry and exit that gave rise to a symmetrical vacuum structure, whose axis had a practically southern orientation (Figure 2.1, above).The great intensity of both jets, of more than 120 knots, caused along its axis an intense cycle rotation, fruit of the great shear existing between the jet of entry to the vacuum, of the northern component, and the one of exit of the southern component.The intense cyclonic rotation with a negative thermal anomaly in the high levels would be the origin of the Isolated Depression of High Levels (dana) that would affect our territory days later. At 2:00 hours on the 24th, an extensive barocline leaf was seen on the right flank of the jet of exit of this cold core, a symptom of the existence of a large-scale dynamic forcing that raised the air masses above the ocean towards the medium-high levels of the atmosphere, thus transporting moisture from lower latitudes to higher latitudes. In the water vapour images it is seen that the extensive barocline leaf (Figure 1, above) evolved throughout the 24th day, acquiring a greater curvature at its tipping point, a symptom that the formation of a surface boresca was occurring and that at the beginning of the 25th day was already fully developed to the southwest of the British islands (Figure 2.1, below).

## 2. Descripción sinóptica.

In Atlantic waters, upstream of the drain, an extensive dorsal was located, which was moving eastwards, thus increasing the circulation of the wind at the height of the north component, which helped to intensify the jet of entry into the void. In turn, its jet of exit was losing intensity after the cyclogenetic process, thus starting an imbalance between the two, favoring the displacement to the south of the depression in height, in which intense rotating activity was maintained.

## 2. Descripción sinóptica.

Throughout the 25th day, depression in height and low surface began to decouple by the displacement of depression to the south, causing the region of low surface pressures to experience a rapid weakening. Thus, the depression was lowered in latitude until it was placed in the early hours of the 27th day on the vertical of the Iberian peninsula, constituting a nucleus of vorticity detached from its empty mother (Figure 2.2, below left). This isolated depression of high levels, without the existence of a system of low surface pressures with closed winds circulation around its center is technically known as dana.

## 2. Descripción sinóptica.

During the 27th day, the existing dorsal upstream advanced towards the northeast, maintaining the intensity of the jet from entry to the dana, but making it change to the northeast component, so that there began to be a change in the direction of displacement from this towards the southwest. Thus, on the 28th at noon, the dana was located on the vertical of the Gulf of Cadiz, still without a region of low pressures with clearly defined center. In the afternoon of the 28th, its displacement continued to the south, located on the vertical of the north coast of Morocco in the early hours of the 29th day. At this moment the jet of entry of the dorsal was weakened by the west-northwest, while its jet of exit was intensified by the interaction of the latter with the subtropical jet, developing an intense southwest circulation whose interaction with the mountainous system of the Atlas led to the formation of a low surface pressure to the undersea of Alborán (Figure 2.3).The formation and subsequent development of this low induced the intensification of the circulation of east component wind on the Mediterranean peninsular facade, bringing moisture to this region. Meanwhile, the jet of southwest component in height induced on the south and the eastern peninsular half an important dynamic forcing that helped to the ascent of the mass of humid air of Mediterranean origin and to the organization of the convection developed after these ascents. In turn, a center of low pressures in surface on the area of the Gulf of Cadiz was formed, so that the dana technically passed to be defined from then on as isolated cold drunkness (BFA).

## 2. Descripción sinóptica.

From the evening of the 29th and throughout the 30th day, the displacement of the current dorsal above the BFA continued towards the European continent, entering the British islands and extending later to the north and center of Europe. With this height pattern, high over the north of Europe and low over the south-west of Europe, a classic pattern of atmospheric blockade, known as the Rex (Rex. 1950) pattern, was configured and maintained the following days.A fundamental feature of the blocking patterns is the establishment of a jet in height of eastern component, which is in general contrary to the prevailing direction of the west in our latitudes. On this occasion, the intensity of the pressure centers in height favored an east jet at the entrance of the BFA that provided sufficient energy for the maintenance of the depression in height (Figure 2.4). On the other hand, during this period of time, a repositioning of the center of the BFA, moving towards the Atlantic, to the west of Portugal, and thus shifting the area of the greatest atmospheric instability towards the southwestern quadrant peninsular.

## 2. Descripción sinóptica.

During the following days, the dorsal that ran along the north of the dana, slowly surrounded it and curving to the south by its eastern flank, acquiring this axis with a northwest-southeastern orientation. This resulted in the jet of entry to the depressive system acquiring a southeast component that contributed to the deformation of the cold core in height and the weakening of the low surface that ended up disappearing in the early hours of Saturday, November 2.From this moment, technically, the depressive structure went back to being defined as dana. On the other hand, the slow decrease of the intensity of the input jet to the southeast component dana, which in the morning of day 3 was 50 knots, as well as the increase of the intensity of its output jet (of almost 80 knots), was shifting its center towards the center-southeastern peninsular (figure 2.5, above), beginning its phase of dissipation.

For each one of them the instant of beginning and end is indicated, the type of structure, the provinces to which it affected or through which it transited, the most adverse surface effects, as well as a brief description. All the hours indicated in this section are referred to the Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) that in winter time is one hour before the official time of the peninsula (2 hours less in summer).The identified convective structures have been labeled according to the classification of convective systems described in Gallis et al. (2008): Belonging to the meso-, meso-, etc. scale. They are characterized by the consecutive development of new cells along the front of the racha. Their average duration is around 1-3 hours.The length of their yuncques is 50-100 km and do not present stratiform precipitation. The possible effects on the surface are hail less than 5 cm, sudden floods, tasted, and lower lendicts of category.A supercell is a single-cell storm that presents a deep and persistent upward current in rotation (mesocyclone) and two downward currents: that of the front flank (FFD), the main and most extensive, and that of the rear flank (RFD), which is smaller and more concentrated. Its average duration is around 1-3 hours. They spread anomalously, shifting to the right of the middle flow (cyclonic or right to move), or to the left (anticyclonic or left to move). Its appearance is ellipsoid in satellite images and 'gancho' or 'coma' in radar images. The length of its anvils is usually in the range 20-150 km. The possible surface effects are hail greater than 2 cm, tornado EF2-EF5, winds greater than 100 km/h, sudden flooding. HP types (High Precipitation), LP (Low Precipitation) and classical. When they form several in the same environment, they are sometimes organized in line or cluster. They can result from a storm.

## Sistemas convectivos de mesoescala (SCM) .

They are storm systems that produce a contiguous (convective or stratiform) precipitation area of the order of 100 km or more in the horizontal scale in at least one direction. They are composed of a large number of individual cells whose proximity causes interactions between them. These individual cells share elements of the cloud system, of the precipitation volume and of the cold air bag ( cold-pool).Its typical duration is 3-24 hours. Due to its size and duration are affected by the acceleration of Coriolis. The length of the semi-highest axis is around 100-500 km. They can be classified into the following subtypes: Each structure has been labelled taking into account the type of convective structure it presents, the day of the month in which it had its greatest impact, and a number indicating its order of formation on the day in question, so that the first structure on 29 October, which was a convective system of mesoscale, is the SCM29-I, while the third of that day, which were multicells, is the MC29-III. In those cases where structures of different types coexist in the same area simultaneously, they will be labeled as a single precipitating structure by the acronyms EP (e. EP28-III).

## 5. Contextualización histórica del evento del 29 de octubre en Valencia.

In the last 70 years the province of Valencia has suffered different episodes of torrential precipitation that have resulted in serious floods. Among the most prominent are those that happened on October 14, 1957, October 20, 1982 and November 3, 1987. All of them represent events that had a great impact on the lives of people and on infrastructures, assuming some of them, such as that of 1982, a turning point for this meteorological service that experienced an important technological advance for its modernization.

## 5. Contextualización histórica del evento del 29 de octubre en Valencia.

In this section it is intended to compare, from the meteorological point of view, the episode of October 29, 2024 with the previous ones to determine the possible similarities and differences between them. In the four analyzed events, the accumulated maximum precipitation occurred over the province of Valencia, although they were located in different areas (Figure 5.1). Thus, in the episode of 1957 the maximum was placed towards the northwest of the capital, causing a flood that flooded the city of Valencia due to the overflow of the river Turia.During the 1982 event, the peak focused on the central-southwestern area of the province, resulting in the well-known 'Tous Pantanada' due to the break-up of the dam that caused serious flooding downstream of the Júcar River. In 1987, the highest rainfall focused on the Safor regions, on 3 November, and on the Ribera Alta region on 4 November. Regarding the 2024 event, the low rainfall throughout the coast stands out, in contrast to the large accumulations of only tens of kilometres inland, focusing on the area of maximum precipitation in the interior of the province and north-western part of the south coastal warning zone. It also highlights the key role of the orography in triggering the convection, defining very well in the precipitation map (Figure 5.1) the region where the convective structure began to develop that would lead to later torrential precipitation (extreme south of the precipitation zone, Figure 5.1) and which corresponds to a mountainous region located in the south-eastern end of the province.

If we focus on the day when the peaks accumulated in each of the episodes occurred, we can observe a similar atmospheric synoptic pattern (Figure 5.2), a cold depression in height or dana towards the peninsular south, centered either on the area of the Straits and North Africa (1982 and 2024), or on the environment of the Gulf of Cadiz (1957 and 1987).As can be seen, the existing baric gradient around the depression in height is greater when its position focuses more on the area of the Strait, causing the formation of a shallower surface over the Alboran Sea and therefore intensifying the circulation of the east over the eastern half of the peninsular. Contrary to the above, there is greater confidence in the climatic projections related to the intensity of the associated precipitation (Pinheiro et al. 2024). One of the main factors associated with this increase is a fundamental physical law that relates the greater capacity of a warmer atmosphere to store a higher moisture content, which gives it greater potential to condense higher concentrations of water vapour and, therefore, cause accumulations of precipitation also higher.This fact has already been reported in Fisher et al. 2016, where the existence, on a global scale, of an upward trend in the intensity of precipitation in recent decades has been reported. In this sense, the expected increase in the intensity of precipitation, from the thermodynamic point of view, would be 7% for each degree of increase in temperature. However, some studies point out that even greater increases are being observed than this percentage, which could be linked to changes in atmospheric dynamics that would result in greater efficiency of precipitation (Mishra and others. 2024). Records observed on a sub-diary scale, such as that of this last episode, could be within this last context of change.

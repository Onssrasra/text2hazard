## Traffic jams and flight delays due to heavy rain and lightning storm in Malaga

 At Malaga Airport, a severe thunderstorm forced air traffic control to implement traffic management measures to ensure safety, causing delays of approximately 15 minutes for flights scheduled during the early morning hours. This type of measure is unusual at Malaga Airport, which is known for operating efficiently even in adverse conditions. Airport sources told ABC that, nevertheless, operations have now returned to normal.

## Forty liters

 The heavy rains that fell early Tuesday morning in the province of Málaga have left more than 40 liters in various locations, including the capital city and the Sierra de Mijas mountain range. According to the Andalusian Regional Government's Hidrosur network, rainfall has been recorded in both inland and coastal areas of the province, as well as in some reservoirs In the last 12 hours, 47.3 liters per square meter have been recorded in the Guadalhorce River; 43.4 liters in the city of Málaga (Paseo de la Farola); 42.9 liters in the town of Pujerra; 41.6 liters in the Sierra de Mijas; 31.3 liters in Torre del Mar, part of Vélez-Málaga; and 31.2 liters in Ronda. Rainfall has also been recorded in some reservoirs, such as Guadalteba, with 42 liters per square meter; Conde del Guadalhorce, with 40.5 liters; Limonero, with 34.8 liters; La Viñuela, with 21.2 liters; and La Concepción, with 16.9 liters per square meter.

## Traffic jams and road closures

 There have also been some traffic jams and road closures in the capital, such as on Lope de Vega Avenue, heading towards the city center, near Julio Cortázar Avenue due to a road subsidence. As the City Council indicated on social media, traffic has been diverted towards Colonia Santa Inés. There is also heavy traffic on the access roads from Juan XXIII Avenue to Plaza Manuel Azaña, Blas Infante Avenue, Guerrero Strachan Avenue, Velázquez Avenue, Camino Suárez, the Azucarera - Interhorce road, Santa Rosa de Lima, and Victoria In addition, there are pools of water on the MA21, near the Churriana intersection, and the intersections of Avenida Herrera Oria-Virgen de las Flores, Pasillo del Matadero Puente del Carmen, Pasillo Santa Isabel - Puente de la Aurora and Avenida Lope de Vega - Atabal have also been affected.

## Orange alert

 The State Meteorological Agency (Aemet) maintains the orange alert active until midday in the Malaga regions of Costa del Sol and Guadalhorce due to storms and rainfall that can reach 30 liters per square meter in one hour and 80 liters accumulated in 12 hours.
